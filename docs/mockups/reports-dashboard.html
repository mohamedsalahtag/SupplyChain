How to read this mockup. The Dashboard becomes the first tab of Reports and opens by default. Every card below is a candidate: tick Keep on the ones you want, the bar at the bottom collects your list. Cards marked now are live (refreshed every minute) and ignore the period; cards marked period follow the Period filter (default: last 12 weeks — the performance query is the heaviest over years). The Company filter and the spec 24 rules apply to every card: your companies only, never a total across units or currencies, N/A when there is nothing to divide by. Each card says who it serves, what question it answers, why it earns its place and where the numbers come from. Recommended marks the set I would build first.
