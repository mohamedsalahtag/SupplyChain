# Training plan

Train each role for 60–90 minutes on the UAT server, in groups of up to 8, using the UAT script as the exercise. Everyone starts on **My work**: that is where the app says what to do next.

## Everyone (10 minutes)
- **My work:** one tab per kind of task, due and overdue, exceptions. The link opens the record, and **Back to My work** returns.
- **Statuses:** hover any status to see who has it and what comes next; "What do the statuses mean?" on each list.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Every change is versioned and shows in History.
- **Comments and attachments** on every record. Comments cannot be edited.

## Sales (45 minutes)
1. A demand is **containers per week**: groups of identical containers, each with its make-up in % (A2–A4).
2. Submit makes **version 1, the baseline** the KPIs are measured against.
3. After acceptance, only **change requests** change a demand: change containers, cancel a week, cancel the demand. Procurement decides.
4. Acknowledge the award, or raise a query.
5. Reports: your demands' execution.

## Procurement (75 minutes)
1. Accept or return demands; merge demands of the same company.
2. RFQs: the origin-filtered supplier shortlist ranked by PO history; the supplier view (CSV); recording quotes.
3. Award by containers: the squares and counts, **+ Add container**, above-offer awards (logged).
4. Shipments and confirmed ETD; shipping terms; **hand off** (with or without Sales' acknowledgement, and why that matters).
5. Returned handoffs: what the PO team asks for and how to fix it.
6. Change requests you raise (not sourced, add quantity, week shift, mix change) and those you decide.

## PO team (60 minutes)
1. Accept or return a handoff: the snapshot is what was sent.
2. PO preparation: pick SKUs only where none was given, split across SKUs, "Not in SAP…".
3. Build, validate, submit. What **SAP outcome unknown** means and why the app never resends blindly (runbook 1, with the stub).
4. Resolving with evidence.

## Administrators and IT (60 minutes)
Operations status, the runbooks, users (roles and companies), Configuration, the restore drill.
