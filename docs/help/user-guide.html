Supply Chain · How the application works

One demand, one quantity ledger, one purchase order per supplier. Sales asks, Procurement sources, the PO team orders in SAP. Every step is done by the department that owns it, and nothing changes after acceptance without a change request decided by the other department. Rule numbers (R1…) are explained on the right and in full on the last page.

Also, at any time after acceptance

  • Change requests (next page): Sales changes containers, cancels a week or the demand; Procurement reports quantity it cannot source, adds quantity a supplier offers, shifts a week or changes the mix. The other department decides.
  • Merge / unmerge (Procurement): weeks of one accepted demand move into another of the same company to be sourced together; undo while the merged quantity is still Open.
  • Release (RFQ) and un-award (award) send quantity back a step, with a reason.

My work: where your steps wait

Every task or exception above appears on My work for the people who hold that step's permission in that company, with a due time from Configuration → Workflow. Items are opened and closed only by the action itself (R12); nobody closes them by hand. Overdue items turn red and are escalated once. The Reports dashboard shows the same queues for the whole company.

Change requests: how a locked demand changes

After Procurement accepts a demand, it is locked. Any change goes through a change request (CR): one department asks, the other department decides, and only then does anything change. Every request, decision and effect is recorded on the demand (Change requests tab, Versions, History). Nobody decides a request they raised, even when they hold both roles.

The cycle, in seven steps

  1. Raise. Make the change in the editor (or pick lines and quantities), choose a reason code and write a comment — both mandatory — then Check & send.
  2. Pre-check (by the server). The changes become items, one per changed group or line, each with its quantity effect per material. Checks: a reduction needs enough cancellable quantity (Open, in RFQ, quoted, awarded, handed off, in PO preparation — never PO submitted or created); a composition change is refused while any quantity of that material is awarded or later ("un-award first"); the materials and week must not be in another open CR; one unit per material per demand. A failed check saves the request as Blocked with the reasons and holds nothing. A passed check makes it Submitted.
  3. Holds. While the request is open, its week and materials are on hold (a badge on the demand and the RFQ): no other CR can touch them, and award, handoff and PO submission wait for the decision. RFQs and quotes continue.
  4. Decide change request appears on the other department's My work. The raiser is told when it is decided, and may withdraw before that (comment required; holds released, nothing changes).
  5. Decision, per item: Approve, Approve less (fewer containers or less quantity) or Reject, plus a decision comment. The request ends Approved, Partially approved or Rejected.
  6. Apply, all or nothing, in the same step as the decision. Approved reductions cancel quantity least-progressed first: Open, then in RFQ, quoted, awarded, handed off, PO preparation. Cancelled quantity stays on the demand as Cancelled. If part of it reached SAP meanwhile, as much as possible is cancelled and the request shows Partially applied. Approved additions add Open quantity (and new materials or weeks when needed). Container counts are updated and a new demand version CR applied is saved. If anything else fails, nothing changes and the decider sees why.
  7. Follow-up. Holds are released and the My work item closes. If cancelled quantity was awarded, Procurement gets Tell the supplier. If it was handed off or accepted by the PO team, the handoff is returned automatically and Procurement reconfirms containers and ETD, then hands off again. Sales' acknowledgement of a changed award goes back to Pending.

How Procurement responds to a Sales request

  • Open it from My work → Decide change request. Each item shows before → after, the quantity effect per material, and the quantity at submit and now — so the impact on RFQs, quotes and awards is visible before deciding.
  • Approve what the market allows; use Approve less when a supplier can take part of the extra containers; Reject with the reason in the comment when the change is no longer possible (quantity already on a PO would have been blocked by the pre-check).
  • After approval, act on the follow-ups: Tell the supplier about cancelled awarded quantity; reconfirm and hand off again when a handoff came back automatically.
  • Response time is measured (Reports → Performance and the Dashboard): the hours from submission to decision, per department.

Change requests: worked examples

1 · The customer reduced the order

D-000230, week W42: 5 containers of Royal Gala S-100 (1,540 CT each). Two containers are still Open, three are quoted in RFQ-000041.

Sales → Change containers: sets the group to 3 containers, reason Customer cancelled or reduced the order, comment. Pre-check: the reduction is 3,080 CT; cancellable quantity is 7,700 CT (Open + quoted) → Submitted; W42 and the material go on hold.

Procurement decides: Approve. Applied: 3,080 CT cancelled least-progressed first — the 3,080 CT Open go first, nothing quoted is touched. W42 now has 3 containers, version CR applied, holds released. The RFQ still asks for its 3 containers.

2 · Cancel a week that is already handed off

D-000228, week W41: 3 containers awarded to Andes Export and handed off (HO-000012, not yet submitted to SAP).

Sales → Cancel week W41, reason Sales forecast changed. Pre-check passes: handed-off quantity is still cancellable.

Procurement approves. Applied: 4,620 CT cancelled; HO-000012 is returned automatically (reason CR_CHANGE), the PO draft in preparation is void, and Procurement gets Handoff returned automatically and Tell the supplier. Had the PO already been submitted to SAP, that part could not be cancelled: the request would end Partially applied and the PO team changes the SAP order by hand.

3 · Nothing at budget: not sourced

D-000226, week W43, Packham pears 70-75 ZA, 1,120 CT Open. Every quote is above budget.

Procurement → Not sourced…: 1,120 CT, reason Quoted prices above budget (a reason that counts against Procurement), and lowers the week to 0 containers.

Sales decides: Approve less — 560 CT, keeping one container in the hope of a later offer. Applied: 560 CT cancelled as not sourced; the not-sourced rate on the reports rises accordingly; 560 CT stay Open and keep raising Open quantity near ETD until they are in an RFQ.

4 · A supplier offers two more containers

RFQ-000041 for D-000231: Agrisouth can ship 2 more containers of Royal Gala in W42.

Procurement → Add quantity…: 3,080 CT Royal Gala S-100 Cat1 CL, W42, plus 2 extra containers, reason Market opportunity. The proposed line appears on the supplier view marked proposed; Agrisouth quotes it; the award waits.

Sales decides each item: quantity Approve less 1,540 CT, containers Approve less 1. Applied: the demand line grows by 1,540 CT of Procurement quantity (outside the execution rate), already Quoted on RFQ-000041; W42 gains 1 container. Procurement can now award it.

5 · The supplier can only ship a week later

RFQ-000039 line: Navel oranges EG, 4,200 CT asked for W42; the supplier offers W43.

Procurement → Propose week shift… to W43 with 3 containers in the RFQ for W43, reason Supply available only in another week. The line shows Pending Sales · CR-…; quotes can still be recorded, the award waits.

Sales approves: the quantity is now approved for W43 and the demand line shows approved for 2026-W43. A rejection would have put the line back to W42.

6 · Blocked at the pre-check

Sales changes the shares of a group whose Fuji apples are already awarded → Blocked: un-award first. Procurement can un-award (keeping the quotes), then Sales raises the request again.

Sales cancels W41 while CR-000012 on W41 is still open → Blocked: already in CR-000012. Wait for that decision or withdraw it.

Sales tries to cancel 500 CT when only 400 CT are still cancellable (the rest is on a PO) → Blocked: cancellable quantity is 400 CT.

Reason codes

Sales: Customer cancelled or reduced the order · Sales forecast changed · Customer changed the specification. Procurement: No supplier could supply in time ⚑ · Quoted prices above budget ⚑ · Market opportunity (extra quantity) · Supply available only in another week. ⚑ = counts against Procurement in the not-sourced rate. Administrators keep the list under Configuration → Reason codes.

Statuses: who has it, what is next

Container capacity. Configuration → Workflow → Container capacity keeps the maximum payload per container for each product (major, optionally sub-major and size). When you add materials to a container group, Capacity per container fills in from it — the most specific match, and for a mixed container the smallest; you can still type another number. Delete draft removes a draft demand that was never accepted (its creator or an administrator). A demand you created yourself can be accepted by you only if you hold both Sales and Procurement; otherwise the page says another Procurement user must accept it.

Filters. Every list has a search box and a Filters section (closed until you open it). Pick values — every list is multi-select and searchable; Sub-major only offers the sub-majors of the chosen majors — then press Apply. Applied filters show as chips beside the button (× removes one) and stay when you open a record and come back. In Demands and RFQs the status is part of the Progress column; the status filter is called Stage.

Progress and "Waiting on". The coloured bar of a demand shows where its quantity is: open, in RFQ (waiting for quotes), quoted (Procurement to award), awarded (Procurement still to hand off), handed off (the PO team to accept), PO preparation (the PO team to submit), on PO, and on PO (simulated) — striped, created by the SAP simulator, not in SAP. Waiting on (a column and a filter in Demands, RFQs and Awards) names the team that acts next and what it does. On the demand page, What's left lists each part that is not finished — per award and supplier — with where it is, who acts next and a link; Procurement: nothing left to do means the rest is with the PO team or Sales. An award (AB-…, award batch) has no status of its own: its page shows the same bar and, under Where it is, each supplier's handoff, PO and next step.

The rules, in one list

  1. R1 Company scope. You see and act only on the companies you work for. Another company's records do not exist for you.
  2. R2 Whole units, one unit. Cartons are never split; one unit per material per demand; quantities are never added across units. Containers are the one measure added across everything.
  3. R3 The baseline. Version 1 at the first submission is the KPI baseline and never changes.
  4. R4 Separation of duties. Whoever created a demand cannot accept it; whoever handed off cannot accept the handoff; nobody decides their own change request.
  5. R5 Locked after acceptance. Nothing on an accepted demand changes without a change request decided by the other department.
  6. R6 Status follows quantity. A demand's status is worked out from its quantity ledger. Nothing is deleted: quantity is cancelled or merged, with history.
  7. R7 Suppliers. An RFQ goes only to suppliers able to supply the origin, not blocked in SAP, and set up for the company's purchasing organization.
  1. R8 Awards are containers. Award whole containers per week; above the offer is allowed but logged; one currency per supplier per award.
  2. R9 Acknowledgement is a heads-up. Sales' acknowledgement never blocks; handing off without it needs a reason and is recorded.
  3. R10 One handoff, one PO. One handoff per award × supplier equals one purchase order; the checklist must be green; a returned handoff puts the quantity back to Awarded.
  4. R11 SAP is never guessed. One live draft per handoff; the payload is frozen at submit; an unknown outcome is looked up by reference and never resent blindly; master data older than the setting (26 h) is shown as a warning; it does not block submission.
  5. R12 My work is written by the actions. Items open and close only through the step itself; due times come from Configuration; overdue items are escalated once.
  6. R13 Holds. An open change request holds its week and materials: award, handoff and PO submission wait; RFQs and quotes continue.
  7. R14 Cancellation order. Approved reductions cancel the least-progressed quantity first; quantity submitted to SAP is never cancelled in the app.
